Nothing starts grouped. The engine's own clustering is printed under each photo as engine cluster N — what it guessed, shown so you can disagree with it. It is not applied.

Group numbers are stable. Once a group has a number it keeps it; deleting a group leaves a gap rather than renumbering the others under you. The numbers are display only — the answer is written in photo numbers, which is what the storage keys on.

What happens after. Each named subject is remembered: next time a photo of it appears it is recognised, not asked about. Only the photos you actually picked are promoted as examples — never a whole cluster. Photos left undecided cost nothing and return at a later checkpoint. Not mine is the only irreversible choice, which is why it writes the word confirm.

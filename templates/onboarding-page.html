Why nothing is pre-selected. The engine ranks what it found by how many files carry it. Measured on a real collection, that ranking put a phone's true screen size sixth, under three sizes that turned out to be forwarded images — and it proposed a hotel as a home, because a two-week trip is also "many separate days, including after dark".
